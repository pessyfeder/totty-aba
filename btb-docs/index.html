How The BTB Method works

At The BTB Method, you learn how to respond to your child's behavior in the moment, with consistent rewards, limits, and follow-through, and that response is what treats the behavior.

Your child's behaviorWhat happens in the moment
→
Your planned responseRewards, limits, follow-through
→
The behavior changesOver time, with consistency
Think of it like a doctor's treatment plan. With medication, the treatment works when it's taken exactly as prescribed. Here the treatment works through behavior, so the plan has to be applied consistently and precisely, week after week. Like a weekly visit to a therapist, regular sessions keep it on track.
It takes commitment: regular sessions, and carrying out the plan between them.

What to expect

  1. Reach out

    Send a short message with your state and the behavior that worries you most. We reply personally and explain what applies in your state.

  2. First session

    Together we choose the one priority behavior, start a simple 7-day baseline, and find what motivates your child.

  3. Baseline, then the plan

    You track for a week, mostly by marking a dot each time the behavior happens. Then we build a plan around your child.

  4. Guidance begins

    You apply the plan, we adjust it each session, and you keep tracking so we can see what works.

Common questions

Who are the sessions with?

With you, the parent, caregiver or educator. The BTB Method is guidance for parents, caregivers and educators. You carry out the plan, with our guidance.

Are sessions in person?

No. Sessions take place by call, text and video, so you can meet from anywhere.

Which states do you serve?

Requirements differ by state. In New Jersey, no diagnosis is needed. In New York, a diagnosis is required. Other states vary, so tell us your state and we will let you know what applies to you.

Do you take insurance?

No. The BTB Method is private pay.

How is The BTB Method different from the ABA my insurance covers?

The BTB Method is ABA, set up with a different structure. You work one-on-one with a licensed behavior analyst, much as you would with a therapist or social worker, and you learn to carry out the plan yourself, in the moments that matter. Insurance-covered ABA is a fine choice for many families. If that's the route you'd rather take, we're happy to talk it through. Another benefit: with The BTB Method, no official diagnosis is needed to get started in New Jersey. Requirements vary by state, so tell us where you live and we will let you know what applies.

Why pay privately instead of using insurance?

Private pay lets your plan, session schedule, and goals follow your child's needs rather than authorizations, billing codes, or paperwork. ABA works best when the plan is carried out consistently and correctly, and when it isn't, families often don't see the results they hoped for. Each session here is built around making that happen across home, school and community. No outcome, result, or timeline is guaranteed.

Can you guarantee results?

No outcome, result, or timeline is guaranteed. Progress depends on many factors, including how consistently the plan is carried out across home, school and community.

Is this for emergencies?

No. If someone is in danger, call 911.
